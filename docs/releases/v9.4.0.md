## What's new in TinyTouch 9.4.0

### 3D Pose mode
A second annotation type next to Touch. When you prepare a new video, choose **Touch** or **3d Pose**.

- Mark when body zones start and stop on the Alternate diagram: **left click** sets ON, **right click** sets OFF, **middle click** or **d** clears it.
- Set **body** and **head** size with sliders. The value carries forward as you step through frames, and the timeline shows both as traces.
- Rate each frame's **quality** (optional).
- Pose labels are stored separately from Touch, and only changed labels are written on save.
- Pose export and Analysis come in a later release; Touch export is unchanged.

### Pick up where you left off
- TinyTouch reopens the project you had open last, on its saved frame. Without one, it shows the video library.
- The video library is sorted by last use and shows the Pose figure for Pose projects.

### Smoother coding
- Touch and Pose playback runs in real time at up to 100 FPS.
- Windows open without flicker.
- The diagram resizes through a live preview and lays out once you let go.

### Safer work
- Warns about touches without an offset before you close the app.
- Interrupted video preparation resumes instead of starting over, and cancelling it works reliably.
- Export failures are reported separately from saves, and Touch export is faster.

### New look
- A baby-hand app icon for the window, taskbar and the .exe.
- A loading screen appears as soon as you open the app.
- No console window behind the app.
- Only one TinyTouch runs per folder; opening it again brings the running window to the front.

## Download
- **Windows 10/11 (64-bit):** download **TinyTouch-v9.4.0-windows-x64.zip**, extract it, and double-click **TinyTouch-v9.4.0.exe**.
- **Linux:** the 9.4.0 Linux build is coming soon. Until then, use the [9.3.0 Linux release](https://github.com/Humanoids-CTU/tiny-touch/releases/tag/v9.3.0).

Touch exports keep the 9.3.0 format, described in the [data format specification](https://github.com/Humanoids-CTU/tiny-touch/blob/master/docs/DATA_FORMAT.md).
